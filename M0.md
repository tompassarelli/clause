# Clause M0 evidence contract

## Constitution, contrast corpus, and parser gate

**Status:** five contrast sets published; explanation/intervention/diff
expansion candidate; no parser or semantic-core change is authorized by this
document

**Authority:** [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) is the milestone authority and
[SURFACE.md](SURFACE.md) is the provisional human-surface direction. This
contract freezes how M0 records evidence without freezing the surface decisions
that the evidence exists to test.

## 1. What this checkpoint fixes

The following are directional invariants:

- semantic symbols participate in role-labelled relations rather than owned
  object fields;
- a focused block is erasable projection sugar over co-equal claims;
- the focused `state locked` line elaborates to the ordinary relation claim
  `focus state locked`;
- `:` denotes binding and never silently denotes membership, a field, equality,
  a block opener, or an ordinary relation assertion;
- enumeration, homogeneous structural binding, and focused-claim blocks are
  distinct forms;
- the lossless reader is semantic-free, while role-labelled elaboration occurs
  in a later stage; and
- the existing Rust grammar and semantic core remain migration/parity evidence.

Three surface axes are deliberately **not** fixed by this checkpoint:

1. `member of` versus another exact membership spelling;
2. canonical indentation width and the reader policy that admits it; and
3. whether a distinct focused projection-binding sugar such as
   `state: locked` should be admitted at all.

No fixture may turn one candidate on those axes into the grammar by calling it
canonical, accepted, invalid, or implementation-ready.

## 2. Corpus contract

The paired files under `clause:corpus/m0/cases/` use:

```text
CASE.clause
CASE.expected.json
```

Each JSON document declares `clause-m0-contrast-v1` and records:

- exact source lines and line-break policy, sufficient to reconstruct the
  source bytes;
- the semantic or structural axis under test;
- fixed requirements and forbidden readings;
- unresolved candidate metadata where the source spelling or layout is not yet
  admitted; and
- an abstract role-labelled elaboration where the semantics are fixed.

Abstract relation handles begin with `$`. They name semantic roles without
choosing a surface spelling or inventing a Store identity. Two cases in the
same `equivalence_group` must have identical `elaborated` values whenever
that field is present.

An unresolved case is evidence input, not an accepted program. Its oracle says,
“if this candidate is admitted, these invariants must hold.” A later M0 ruling
must retain the case and add the measured verdict; it may not silently bless or
delete the losing contrast.

## 3. Reader and stage boundary

The eventual pipeline remains:

```text
UTF-8 bytes
  -> Stage A: lossless concrete tree + source/layout diagnostics
  -> Stage B: structural block classes + classification diagnostics
  -> Stage C: exact scoped phrase and recursive-term resolution
  -> Stage D: role-labelled authored judgments
  -> current checked semantic core where honest
```

Stage A must retain every source byte, line break, delimiter, token, trivia
range, and diagnostic without deciding object, type, membership, relation, law,
or query meaning. Stage B may classify child forms only after a reviewed layout
policy has supplied one group tree. Stage C never uses probabilistic NLP.

This checkpoint does not choose a fixed-width or arbitrary-column layout
algorithm. The paired two- and four-space cases ensure that the choice is made
explicitly and that layout normalization cannot change the co-equal role graph.

## 4. Precedent boundary

The conventional components are documented by primary sources:

- [Python indentation stacks](https://docs.python.org/3/reference/lexical_analysis.html#indentation);
- [Haskell layout expansion](https://www.haskell.org/onlinereport/lexemes.html#sect2.7);
- [Agda mixfix and fixity resolution](https://agda.readthedocs.io/en/latest/language/mixfix-operators.html);
- [rust-analyzer's full-fidelity syntax trees](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust-analyzer/blob/master/docs/book/src/contributing/syntax.md); and
- [rustc paired expected-output tests](https://rustc-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/tests/compiletest.html).

Clause's decision is not to invent those mechanisms. The novelty under test is
their composition with form-inferred blocks, exact recursive phrase shapes,
erasable focus, and stable role-labelled semantics.

## 5. Retained contrast sets

### 5.1 Foundational surface set

The first set contains:

- the controlling co-equal focus specimen at two and four spaces;
- expanded equivalents using `member of` and symbolic membership candidates;
- enumeration at two and four spaces;
- a homogeneous value-shape binding block;
- legacy `thing: Space`, which is binding and never membership; and
- focused `state: locked`, whose colon is binding while admission and checked
  carrier remain unresolved, and which is forbidden from becoming the ordinary
  `state` relation claim.

### 5.2 Relation-shape set

The second set adds:

- a compact `connects` schema candidate with three named, domain-bound roles
  and one operational projection/cardinality contract;
- a focused functional `position` contract that remains a relation contract,
  never a field declaration;
- focused, expanded, and explicit structural renderings of one `connects`
  claim with an identical role graph;
- one deliberately overlapping pair of exact phrase shapes whose diagnostic
  must name both candidates and the conflicting focus-role domains; and
- one root fragment missing the required focus role, which must diagnose that
  role rather than invent an implicit object.

Schema, arrow, cardinality, and structural-escape spellings remain candidates.
Their role names, participant domains, projection orientation, co-equal claim
semantics, and exact ambiguity obligations are the evidence fixed here.

### 5.3 Recursive-term set

The third set adds:

- a colon-bound pure distance definition whose pattern and body contain named
  holes and nested projection terms;
- a plain binding whose value is a one-valued relation projection;
- a relation claim whose destination role contains a grouped addition of two
  projections;
- grouped and ungrouped overlap expressions that reach the same checked tree
  only because domain checking rejects the alternate grouping; and
- left-grouped, right-grouped, and ungrouped mixfix terms where both groupings
  remain type-correct, so the ungrouped form must diagnose ambiguity.

This set does not publish a global precedence table. Parentheses are retained
in canonical evidence, every eligible role contains a recursive term tree, and
an ungrouped form is accepted only when one checked elaboration survives.

### 5.4 Holes, laws, and selection set

The fourth set adds:

- naked clauses with anonymous, named, and multiple named holes;
- two anonymous holes in one clause, which must receive distinct identities;
- a multi-clause `select` whose repeated named hole correlates the clauses but
  is not projected;
- `any` existence, exact-one selection, and canonical-first selection as three
  distinct result contracts;
- an `if`-inferred positive law with repeated named holes across conclusion
  and premises; and
- the same semantic law with an optional colon-bound human label that does not
  replace its stable hidden identity.

Expected rows use one explicit finite fact context and canonical ordering. They
are semantic oracles, not permission for universal or unbounded logic search.
Random witness choice is absent from every pure query judgment.

### 5.5 Revision ancestry and signed-delta set

The fifth set adds:

- a successor Revision with one exact withdrawal in the reset surface;
- a renamed human header with the same base, Delta, and semantic Revision;
- the current `Revision`/`from:`/`withdraw:` profile as migration-parity
  evidence, not a target candidate;
- reset and current-profile forms of one exact admission;
- one successor containing both an admission and a withdrawal;
- exact diagnostics for an unknown base, nonexistent withdrawal, existing
  admission, and overlapping signed changes; and
- a forward `diff base -> successor` request whose resolved Revision identities
  and authored assertion difference preserve direction.

Signed changes are atomic and ground. The base is an exact Revision identity,
the human header is a separate binding, and equivalent direct/current-profile
forms produce the same content-derived successor identity. These fixtures do
not admit a particular header, sign, arrow, or indentation spelling.

### 5.6 Explanation, intervention, and semantic-diff set

The sixth set adds:

- reset and current-profile forms of one Revision-scoped explanation query;
- a complete frontier of two inclusion-minimal supports with retained proof
  structure;
- reset and current-profile forms of complete `prevent all minimal` and
  `achieve all minimal` intervention queries over one named finite relation
  basis;
- explicit candidate-bound exhaustion results that retain only individually
  certified interventions and never claim a complete frontier; and
- a layered semantic diff separating authored assertion, entailment, selected
  proof, and minimal-support changes across exact Revisions.

Explanation and intervention spellings remain candidates. Fixed here are
Revision scope, proof/support identity, inclusion-minimality, typed finite
bases, deterministic prefixes, honest completeness, and diff-layer separation.

This is not the complete M0 corpus. Complete relation-schema coverage, recursive
terms, holes, laws, selection, Revisions, transitions, effects, hospital
egress, and the one-coin game remain required by [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) before
M0 exits. The relation-schema requirement is only foundationally represented by
the second set; it is not a complete precedence, import, or rename corpus. The
Revision requirement is likewise only foundationally represented by the fifth
set; lineage chains, structural rendering, and declaration-change comparisons
remain outside this checkpoint.

## 6. Acceptance gate

This checkpoint is reviewable only when:

1. exactly fifty-four source/expectation pairs exist;
2. every JSON parses and declares `clause-m0-contrast-v1`;
3. its recorded lines reconstruct the exact paired source bytes;
4. all four co-equal focus/expanded candidates carry the same elaborated role
   graph;
5. the two enumeration candidates carry the same abstract membership graph;
6. `legacy-colon-binding` requires one binding and forbids membership;
7. `focused-colon-binding` remains unresolved, requires colon to mean binding,
   and forbids both object-field and ordinary relation-claim readings;
8. no fixture declares a membership spelling, indentation width, or focused
   projection-binding sugar canonical or accepted;
9. the compact schema preserves three named roles, role domains, exact phrase
   segments, and the declared projection/cardinality contract;
10. focused, expanded, and explicit structural `connects` cases have identical
    role graphs;
11. the overlapping-shape case has no elaboration and names both candidates
    plus the conflicting focus-role domains;
12. the missing-focus case has no elaboration and names the absent `door` role;
13. the distance definition is a binding whose pattern and body are recursive
    trees and whose named holes retain identity;
14. projection and nested-role cases retain relation identities and named roles
    at every tree level;
15. grouped and type-disambiguated overlap cases have identical checked trees,
    while the rejected grouping carries an exact domain conflict;
16. left- and right-grouped mixfix cases remain distinct, retain their
    parentheses canonically, and the ungrouped case reports both candidates
    without elaborating;
17. every anonymous hole has a fresh identity, every repeated named hole keeps
    one identity, and named output columns preserve the authored result name;
18. naked selection projects all holes, while explicit `select` projects only
    listed holes and keeps correlated holes internal;
19. `any` returns only Bool, exact-one rejects non-unit cardinality, and
    canonical-first is deterministic rather than random;
20. inferred and labelled law cases have identical semantic-law trees, while
    the label remains a separate binding;
21. reset, renamed, and current-profile withdrawal cases carry the same exact
    base, Delta, and successor identity while keeping surface labels separate;
22. reset and current-profile admission cases carry the same exact base, Delta,
    and successor identity;
23. a mixed successor retains one admission and one withdrawal and applies
    them atomically rather than by incidental source order;
24. unknown-base, missing-withdrawal, existing-admission, and overlap cases do
    not elaborate and report distinct exact diagnostics;
25. `diff` resolves exact base and successor identities, preserves the authored
    direction, and reports the expected signed assertion difference;
26. current-profile fixtures are migration evidence and are never marked as
    target candidates or canonical spellings;
27. reset and current-profile explanation cases have identical resolved
    Revision, target, proof roots, and complete minimal-support frontier;
28. reset and current-profile prevention and achievement cases retain identical
    exact bases, finite relation bases, and complete inclusion-minimal antichains;
29. candidate-bound exhaustion is explicitly incomplete, retains only
    individually verified results, and never infers frontier completeness;
30. semantic diff keeps authored, entailed, selected-proof, and support changes
    distinct while retaining support-frontier completeness status;
31. no Rust, Cargo, current example, test, or generated artifact changes;
32. links, JSON, and diff hygiene pass; and
33. an independent reviewer examines one exact commit before any publication.

The next safe action after review is corpus expansion and a read-only fixture
checker. Parser work remains forbidden until the complete M0 contract and
corpus receive independent review.
